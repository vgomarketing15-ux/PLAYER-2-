      Questions?

      Straight answers. If yours isn't here, ask it on the Recon Call.

      What is Player Two Growth House?+

      A small, hands-on growth partner for owner-run local service businesses. We find where your business is losing money, such as missed calls, unchased quotes, missing reviews and one-time customers, and build the systems that fix it. We diagnose first and build second.

      How is this different from a marketing agency?+

      We don't start with a package or a promise of more leads. We start with a Recon Call, write a proposal from what we found, fix the leaks in the leads you already get before adding new ones, and report what changed in plain numbers.

      How much does it cost?+

      Needed: pricing answer (awaiting sign-off)

      What is a missed-call text back?+

      An automatic text sent from your business number to anyone whose call went unanswered, with a link to book a time. A missed call becomes a lead instead of a lost customer. It's part of The Respawn Protocol.

      What does the Leak Report number mean?+

      It's an estimate built only from the numbers you enter: what's at stake each month from missed calls and quotes nobody followed up, at your usual win rate. Not every caller would have become a job, so treat it as a starting point for the conversation, not a promise.

      Is this an AI thing?+

      No. AI is one of three areas we work in, alongside marketing and operations, and we only use it on a specific problem we've already found. Most businesses need their calls, quotes and follow-ups fixed first.

      Do you do social media posting?+

      Not by default. Most owner-run businesses need a working lead and follow-up system more urgently than a content calendar. If you want posting, we scope it separately.

      What happens on the Recon Call?+

      We ask how the business makes money, where customers come from, what breaks day to day and what you've already tried. We don't recommend anything until we understand the business.

      How will I know it's working?+

      At the Check-In we measure results against what the roadmap promised and show you the numbers. Ongoing work comes with a one-page monthly report.
